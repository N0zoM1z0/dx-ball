Unknown declarations, historical identifiers and build organization are things

This policy supersedes blanket prohibitions on identifier, declaration/definition

not permanent bans on a different bounded candidate or a declared combination.
No new original-game operation or independently recovered declaration is needed

## Allowed source candidates

Search finite, recorded alternatives in natural typed C/C++: meaningful variable
and parameter names, declaration order/grouping, whole-function/type definition
order, genuine scopes and lifetimes of existing objects, and equivalent source
forms consistent with the observed operation contract. Include/header order and
translation-unit ownership/splitting may also be reconstructed as bounded
hypotheses; account for linkage, initialization, ABI and every affected owner.
Keep one implementation shared by legacy and portable builds.
Preserve ABI, widths, ownership, actual state writes, required call order and
defined arithmetic behavior. Do not claim a successful candidate is the unique
historical source merely because its complete compiler output matches.

Existing compiler observations may guide offline constraint solving and pruning.
Keep identifier-hash traversal, sequential frontend IDs and C2 operand ranking
distinct: a name-only search is not automatically useful for an ID-rank residual.
Likewise, names cannot move a local outside its scope's allocation range.
Do not make new compiler/provider observations merely to repeat an answered fact.

The following remain prohibited: copied machine code, assembly, fake or unused
locals/types/declarations inserted only to shift counters, padding introduced
only to match storage, profile-specific game bodies, patched compiler output,
weakened comparisons or masks, and selective mixing of old objects compiled from
different shared inputs.

The pinned toolchain and maintained recipe remain the default, not proof of the
unknown original build setup. Finite hypotheses about language mode or compiler/
linker settings are allowed when tied to a concrete binary residual. Isolate and
record each actual recipe, compare its complete affected closure, and update all
input/flag receipts if adopting it. Do not silently change recipes, sweep random
flags, replace the toolchain or weaken the acceptance standard to gain a match.

## Execute a bounded family

### Exploration and adoption are different checkpoints

The user reaffirmed that the existing EXE/resources are the reconstruction
inputs, not a reason to wait for an original project. Unknown original names,
declarations, compiler choices and module boundaries may be inferred and tested.
An earlier local process rule may be simplified under this authorization; do
not turn an optional workflow convention into another permission requirement.
This does not authorize changing the target, fabricating evidence or relaxing
the definition of byte-exactness.

A private probe may compile and compare only the proposed new module first.
Record that limited scope explicitly: its result is exploratory, not a promotion
or preservation claim for the remaining owner. If that probe is rejected,
building its unchanged companion proposal is unnecessary. If it is promising,
compile the matching remainder and compare the complete affected closure before
adoption. All adopted objects must come from one coherent set of actual inputs;
never substitute a winning probe while silently retaining incompatible old
companions. Reuse successful producer outputs when extending the comparison.

Before execution, record the target residual and relevant retained evidence,
candidate dimensions/domains, finite candidate and compiler-attempt limits,
resource/time limits, affected compiler-input closure and stopping criteria.
Prefer offline filtering over compiling every permutation. A finite search may
test inferred spelling/layout without fresh historical-source proof; it must
still have a concrete mechanism or observable residual to investigate.

The agent may choose the finite domains, useful batch size and stopping limits
within the existing host resource caps, then select the next distinct family
without asking for permission again. A concise recorded plan is sufficient;
another user-approved plan, new provider capture or elaborate new test harness
is not a mandatory gate for routine candidates.

### Proportionate execution, not accumulating gates

Keep the routine record small: the hypothesis and finite limits, actual input/
flag identities, producer status, complete affected-unit results and disposition.
Existing scripts and receipts are the default. Do not require a new bespoke
runner, independent reviewer, multi-stage approval or whole-workspace snapshot
before every candidate or adoption. Reviewers and extra checks are optional
tools for a concrete uncertainty, not prerequisites for continuing work.

Verify inputs and previous acceptances relevant to the affected closure. An
ever-growing union of historical file seals or recursive archive audits is not
a new acceptance requirement. Preserve historical receipts without rewriting
them; record current changed preconditions separately. Documentation-only edits
do not require another compiler run or replay of unchanged old bodies. Keep
complete-unit, relocation and local-target checks strict for any new exact claim.

For adoption, retain a recoverable copy of files actually being overwritten and
update the actual owners, recipes and claim ledgers. Use the smallest affected
maintained build/check needed for the change; tests remain optional evidence.
Do not turn an unchanged full-game queue, unrelated cache review or exhaustive
historical revalidation into a delivery gate. If a helper fails, repair it and
continue from the retained producer output rather than restarting the workflow.

Freeze the baseline and each selected candidate's complete recursive inputs and
flags. Compile each distinct input/flag digest once. Reuse archived results for
identical digests, including failures; an unchanged closed control is not a new
experiment. A prior model may participate in a newly bounded combined search,
but record the actual changed inputs and use its retained result as the baseline.
Do not turn one failed family into an unbounded automatic enumeration.

Compare every complete affected candidate and accepted companion, including
transitively affected header owners, actual relocations and local targets, full
literals and attached metadata. Report all promotions, demotions and unchanged
identities. A private target-only match is not a maintained exact. Evaluate whole
coherent closures toward the 270-function objective; temporary private losses
are not by themselves a reason to prohibit investigation, but never hide losses
or combine only the winning old objects. Adoption requires the complete closure
and updated, truthful claim ledgers plus proportionate affected-behavior checks.

Use the existing one-session, one-CPU, CPU35%, memory384/512MiB limits and REA
heap policy. Restore canonical inputs/products safely after rejected trials.
Archive unique closed evidence, verify every member's size/hash and retain
recovery metadata before deleting the exact obsolete product/input list.
Preserve current tested products, useful caches, originals and tools.

## Recover tooling without restarting successful producers

A helper, COFF mapping, report schema or stale preparation seal can fail without
the compiler having failed. Preserve the actual output and failure record,
repair the local helper or prove a complete mapping offline, and continue from
that output. Do not recompile an unchanged digest merely to fix bookkeeping.
Preparation that never launches a compiler is not a consumed compiler attempt.
When a documentation-only change invalidates a preparation's documentation
seals, retain that preparation and review refreshed preconditions; unchanged
frozen sources, baseline objects and retained results remain reusable. Do not
silently rewrite historical receipts or bypass byte/relocation/local-target guards.

## Continue without repeating the permission blocker

After a batch, record what it ruled out and select the next eligible bounded
family. Reassess candidates from current evidence rather than treating an old
"closed" label, unknown historical spelling or missing original materials as
a reason to request the same authorization or mark the goal blocked.
Do not rerun unchanged trials or claim that exhausted finite domains prove
source optimality. Report genuine external/authority blockers separately;
this policy does not promise that every candidate family will yield an exact.
